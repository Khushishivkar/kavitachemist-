Who We Are

Born from a Pharmacy Family

Kavita Chemist was founded by a family that has run a local pharmacy in Mumbai for over 30 years. We understood the daily struggles — manual stock registers, expired medicines lost to poor tracking, and the constant anxiety of not knowing what's in stock.

We built this platform to solve our own problems first, and then shared it with other pharmacy owners who faced the same challenges. Today, we proudly serve over 500 chemist shops across India.
